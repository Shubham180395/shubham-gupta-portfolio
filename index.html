AI Video Model Price Index & Budget Planner

A self-serve pricing intelligence tool that turns inconsistent, credit-based AI video pricing into one comparable $/second index across 18 storefronts — plus a subscription-aware planner for scoping real projects.

The problem

Every reseller — Adobe Firefly, Runway, Freepik, ElevenLabs and more — prices its own credit system differently: different credit costs, clip lengths and resolution tiers, some hidden until checkout. There was no way to answer what will this actually cost me? without reverse-engineering a dozen pricing pages. I did that normalisation once, transparently, with the credit arithmetic visible on every rate.

  • Three views By model, by storefront, and a full heatmap grid of every rate at once.
  • Real subscription maths Budget = max(usage cost, plan floor × billing cycles) — a 3-day project still costs a month's plan.
  • Currency-aware Live USD ⇄ INR with an editable FX rate and an 18% GST toggle.
  • Benchmarked & sourced Markup vs. each lab's direct API, and every cheapest rate links to its source.
  • Data modelling
  • Financial logic
  • Information design
  • Vanilla HTML · CSS · JS
  • No framework, no build step
